{#- One member per row -- the infinite-scroll fragment for the admin panel. Included for the first server-rendered page by `admin/index.html` and re-served bare by `GET /views/admin/fragment` for every page after it, exactly like `challenge/_challenge_cards.html`. It must therefore stay free of anything but the rows themselves: no wrapper, no empty state, no counter -- the fragment endpoint's response *is* this file's output, and `createInfiniteScroller()` counts its top-level children to advance the offset. The row is a **link into the member's profile**, not a control that acts in place. It used to open a role sheet; the profile is where that now lives, because the profile is where the evidence is -- the account's details, its age, the two counters -- and administering someone from a list of thirty near-identical rows is how the wrong person gets changed. A row is therefore an ordinary ``: middle-click, long-press and «باز کردن در تب جدید» all work, which is precisely what an operator holding two support requests wants and what a `role="button"` div cannot give. (That the profile route exists for an admin at all is one clause -- `profile_visibility_filter` in routers/user.py.) -#} {% for member in members %}
