{% extends "layout.html" %} {#- Managing the group. **Five questions, five groups of rows, one shape.** The screen used to be a stack of tall glass cards, each carrying a two-line paragraph and a chevron, and the trouble with that was not density -- it was that a card reading «مشخصات گروه — نام، توضیح، نوع و نشان گروه» does not look like a thing you can change. It looks like a page you might visit. So this is a settings screen and nothing else: one row per *editable value*, the value itself on the row, and the mark of what tapping does at the end of it -- a pencil for "this opens an editor", a chevron for "this opens a page", an icon button for "this does something right here". That last distinction is the whole redesign. Every action on this page is a small icon button whose meaning is the glyph: `+` adds, `✓` and `✕` answer a request, the copy mark copies a link, `↻` replaces the public one. The sentences that used to explain each button are gone from the screen and live in `aria-label`/`title`, which is where a label nobody needs to read twice belongs. The «؟» explainers stay: they answer *concepts* (what a link admits, what a request is), which is a different question from what a control does. The queue is rendered directly here -- it has no paging of its own and no terminal states to filter between, so the screen that used to hold it (`/requests`, with a `?status=` filter) is gone; only `MAX_PENDING_ON_MANAGE` rows show, with a note if the queue runs longer. A page of its own rather than an admin-only section of the group page, for the reason the moderation roster is one: this is where somebody goes to *act*, and a member has no business reading a list of other people's pending requests.
