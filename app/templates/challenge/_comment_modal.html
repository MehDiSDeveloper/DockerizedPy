{#- گفتگو — the conversation, as a modal. Included once by every screen that shows a comment control (the challenge list, the group's challenge list, challenge-detail). It ships *empty*: the threads, the header and the composer's subject are all filled in by `initCommentModal()` in `static/js/comments.js` when a control is tapped, because one screen carries many challenges and rendering a conversation per card is not a thing a page can afford. It is a modal rather than a page for the reason it comes up from the bottom rather than fading in: reading what people said about a challenge is not leaving the list you were scrolling, and a page would take the reader's place with it. Every part of it already exists — the scrim and the slide-up are `createSheet`'s (`.sheet-backdrop`, `sheetSlideUp`), the threads are the same `/fragment` markup `createInfiniteScroller()` has always appended, and the composer is the one this app already had. A single instance per page, keyed on nothing: the modal is one at a time by definition, so its ids are page-unique and the JS holds no registry. The emoji keyboard's catalogue is *not* loaded with the page — it is 14 KB for a control most readers never open, and this partial now rides along on screens that are not about writing at all. `comments.js` fetches it the first time somebody opens the keyboard. -#}
